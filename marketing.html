FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from business owners exploring our marketing services.

Get Your Questions Answered
Do you need GoHighLevel to use your marketing services?+
Not necessarily, but it's highly recommended. Our marketing services are designed to integrate seamlessly with GHL so every lead flows automatically into your pipeline. If you don't have GHL, we'll recommend the right setup as part of onboarding.
What's the minimum ad budget you recommend?+
We typically recommend a minimum of $1,500/month in ad spend for meaningful results. That said, we've helped clients generate strong ROI with as little as $500/month depending on the niche and offer. We'll give you a realistic projection on our strategy call.
How long until we see results?+
Typically 2-4 weeks for early indicators and 60-90 days for consistent, optimized results. Paid ads can produce leads within 48-72 hours of launch; SEO takes 3-6 months to compound. We'll set clear expectations in your 90-day strategy document.
Do you write the ad copy and creative?+
Yes. Our service includes full copywriting and creative direction. We handle the scripts, headlines, body copy, and visual concepts. You review and approve before anything goes live. We specialize in direct-response copy that converts.
Can you work with our existing marketing team?+
Absolutely. Many clients bring us in alongside their existing team to provide strategic oversight, GHL integration expertise, and campaign optimization. We can work as a fractional CMO, a specialist contractor, or a hands-on execution partner.
