Common Questions

Straight Answers

Everything you need to know before booking your strategy call, answered straight.

Get Your Questions Answered
Why are custom code-based builds about 3× the GHL price?+
Real design and engineering from scratch, featuring bespoke layouts, custom animations, performance tuning, and integrations, versus building on GHL's existing system. You pay for differentiation, speed, full code ownership, and scalability. GHL builds are excellent; custom builds are exceptional.
Will my GHL build still connect to my CRM and automations?+
Yes, natively. Forms, bookings, and steps are wired to your pipelines and follow-up from day one. Custom builds also connect to GoHighLevel for lead capture; you just get a bespoke front end that your competitors can't copy.
How is pricing decided?+
By page count for websites and step count for funnels. Need more than the top tier? The "More" option scales at a clear per-page / per-step rate with no hidden surprises. Scope and exact timeline are confirmed on your strategy call.
What's included in every tier?+
Mobile-responsive design, on-page SEO basics, fast-loading images, a lead form connected to your GHL CRM, analytics & pixel setup, SSL, revision rounds (1–3 by tier), pre-launch QA across devices, and a Loom handoff walkthrough. The price is a complete service, not a bare build.
How long does it take?+
GHL sites ~1–3 weeks; GHL funnels ~4–14 business days; custom sites ~2.5–9 weeks; custom funnels ~2–7 weeks. Exact timeline is confirmed on your strategy call. Rush delivery is available for a surcharge.
Do I own everything after the build?+
Yes. GHL builds live inside your GoHighLevel account and they are yours to keep. Custom builds are deployed to your hosting with the full codebase handed to you. You are never dependent on us to keep your site running.
What about ongoing help after launch?+
Step into a Starter–Scale SaaS plan to keep your whole CRM system running and following up automatically. Every build buyer gets priority access.
Do you write the copy?+
Lower tiers include guided copywriting where you supply raw content and we structure and polish it. Higher tiers include done-for-you copy on key conversion pages. A full done-for-you copywriting upgrade is available on any tier.
