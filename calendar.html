Frequently Asked Questions

Is the strategy call really free?+
100% free, no strings attached. We offer this call because most people leave it with massive clarity on their business and many choose to work with us as a result. Even if you don't, you'll walk away with real, actionable insights.
Do I need to have GoHighLevel already?+
No. We work with clients at all stages, from businesses that have never touched a CRM to those with an existing GHL account that needs restructuring. We'll assess where you are and recommend the right next step.
How long does the call take?+
We block 30 minutes for every strategy call. Some go a little longer if there's a lot to cover; we never cut you short. We want you to feel heard and leave with clarity.
What happens after the call?+
You'll receive a follow-up email with a summary of recommendations and, if you're interested in moving forward, a custom proposal within 24 hours. No pressure; we follow up once and then let you decide on your timeline.
