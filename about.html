The Story

From Startup Founder to Building Your System

Chris Karageuzian co-founded and successfully exited an AI-powered lending company. That journey, from scrappy startup to scaled exit, taught him exactly what separates businesses that thrive from those that grind to a halt.

Today, Chris brings that institutional-level operational knowledge to entrepreneurs and growing businesses. His philosophy is simple: it's not about doing more; it's about doing the right things, faster, cleaner, and with a strategy that works.

Chris Alchemy Consulting exists to give every client the same unfair advantage that scaled companies have: bulletproof systems, automated workflows, and a CRM that generates revenue while you sleep.
